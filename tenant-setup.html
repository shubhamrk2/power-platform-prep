Why this route, and why the other one failed#

The Microsoft 365 Developer Program is not available to you. It returned "You don't currently qualify for a sandbox subscription" — Microsoft narrowed that program to effectively require a Visual Studio subscription. It rejects most applicants now regardless of what they enter. Don't retry it; it isn't a form you can fill in better.

The E5 trial is the better route anyway. It gives you the thing that actually matters: global admin of your own tenant. That's what unlocks tenant-level DLP authoring and a full CoE Kit deployment — the two weakest items on your scorecard, and the two you could never practise at T-Mobile because you were a maker inside someone else's governed tenant, not an admin.

Signup friction you already cleared, recorded in case you repeat it:

  • Mastercard rejected as "invalid card number" — a Visa went through
  • The final page appeared to stall after submit; it had actually succeeded
  • PAN was requested — normal for Indian GST compliance on Microsoft subscriptions

⚠️ The one thing that must not slip#

Cancel by 1 October 2026. The trial auto-converts to paid.

  • Calendar reminder set for 25 September — five days of buffer
  • Cancel at https://admin.microsoft.com → Billing → Your products → your E5 subscription → Cancel
  • Cancelling still leaves the tenant usable until the term ends; you don't lose the labs by cancelling early
A second consequence of the 30-day clock: don't start the trial and then delay the labs. Everything in Days 2–4 should happen this week while the tenant is live. This is also why Copilot Studio waits until Day 4 — its own trial clock starts when you activate it, and you want it running during your interview window, not expired.

Immediate next steps#

1. Verify tenant admin in the browser (2 min)#

Sign in to https://admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com and confirm Policies → Data policies is visible in the left nav.

If it's there, DLP authoring works. Combined with Get-DlpPolicy returning {}, that's your confirmation from both directions.

2. Create three environments (15 min)#

Admin Center → Environments → New:

NameTypeDataverse
DEVSandboxYes
TESTSandboxYes
PRODProductionYes
⚠️ If you hit a capacity error: trial tenants have limited Dataverse capacity. Three environments is enough — put CoE Kit in DEV rather than creating a fourth. Check Admin Center → Resources → Capacity to see what's consuming it.

For interview: "In my own tenant I used DEV/TEST/PROD; at T-Mobile it was DEV/STG/UAT/PROD. The principle either way is that production is managed-only and nobody customises directly in it."

3. Install PAC CLI (10 min)#

powershell
# Needs .NET SDK 6+ — check first
dotnet --version

# If missing: https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download
dotnet tool install --global Microsoft.PowerApps.CLI.Tool

pac help

Then once DEV exists:

powershell
pac auth create --environment https://<yourorg>.crm.dynamics.com
pac auth list
pac org who

pac org who returning your identity is the Day 1 finish line.

⚠️ If PAC CLI auth hangs: MFA is likely the cause. Let the interactive browser flow complete rather than expecting silent sign-in. If your machine auto-signs-in with a work identity, use an InPrivate window or pass --username explicitly.

4. Admin PowerShell — already done ✅#

powershell
Install-Module -Name Microsoft.PowerApps.Administration.PowerShell -Force -Scope CurrentUser
Add-PowerAppsAccount
Get-DlpPolicy          # returned {} — confirmed working

Add the second module when convenient:

powershell
Install-Module -Name Microsoft.PowerApps.PowerShell -AllowClobber -Force -Scope CurrentUser
Get-AdminPowerAppEnvironment

The other two time-gated items#

These sit on other people's clocks too. Ten minutes each, worth doing today.

Accenture internal job board#

Check for a comparable Power Platform Senior Consultant role inside Accenture.

Why: your delivery record is already visible internally, internal moves often skip interview rounds, you keep tenure and benefits, and you have a live reference in the T-Mobile EBC administrator.

Why apply externally anyway: an external offer is the strongest salary-negotiation lever that exists — internally or externally. Internal promotions are usually capped at a fixed band, and you said the goal is a meaningful increase.
Sequence: apply externally first. Raise internal interest only once you have an offer, unless your relationship with your career counsellor makes early disclosure safe. In some firms signalling that you're looking carries real risk. You know that relationship; I don't.
